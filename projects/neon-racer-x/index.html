Neon Racer X

A futuristic racing game prototype built around speed, visual atmosphere, and arcade-inspired gameplay.

What it is

A futuristic racing game project built around speed, visual atmosphere, and arcade-inspired gameplay. Neon Racer X focuses on the visceral feeling of high-speed movement through neon-lit environments.

The concept

Exploring speed, visual atmosphere, and arcade-inspired handling. The project experiments with how lighting, motion blur, and track design can create a sense of velocity and immersion in a racing context.

Technology

Game Development Blender

Development

Built in 2024 as a solo project over approximately two months. The core racing mechanics and visual style were developed, then the project was paused to focus on other priorities.
