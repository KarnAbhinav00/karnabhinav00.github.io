Kãl

What it is

A dark, atmospheric game concept combining immersive environments, storytelling, and supernatural horror. Kãl explores the intersection of narrative design and environmental mood to create a deeply unsettling experience.

The concept

Experimenting with atmosphere and supernatural horror storytelling in games. The focus is on environmental storytelling — using light, sound, architecture, and pacing to build tension rather than relying on jump scares or explicit horror.

Technology

Blender Game Design

Development

Started in 2026 as a solo concept project. Currently in the early conceptual phase — defining the world, core mechanics, and visual language. Active development is in progress.
