About

Building through curiosity

I'm Abhinav Karn — a developer building software, exploring AI systems, and learning through implementation. I work on AI assistants, web applications, developer tools, and experimental technology.

I believe the best way to understand technology is to build with it. Most of my projects start as experiments — ideas I want to test, problems I want to solve, or systems I want to understand from the inside.

My work spans local-first AI (running models on personal hardware), browser-based utilities, game prototyping, and assistive technology. Everything I build is hands-on, practical, and driven by genuine curiosity.
