Inspire MANAK Assist Vision

An assistive technology prototype using computer vision and embedded hardware to help visually impaired users.

What it is

An assistive technology prototype exploring computer vision, embedded hardware, wireless communication, and audio feedback. The project focuses on helping visually impaired users navigate and understand their environment through technology.

The problem

Helping visually impaired users navigate and understand their environment through technology. Existing solutions are often expensive, cumbersome, or limited in capability. This project explores a more accessible, integrated approach.

How it works

The system uses real-time computer vision for object detection and scene understanding. An embedded processor handles the vision pipeline, and results are delivered to the user through audio feedback over wireless communication links.

Technology

Computer Vision Embedded Hardware Wireless Communication Audio Feedback

Development

Completed in 2024 as a school project. Developed by a team of three over approximately six weeks, from initial research through working prototype.
